Frequently asked questions

Do you work with video and photography?
Yes, both formats are part of the service and are normally planned together within a single production to make the most of each session.
Can you produce content for social media?
Yes, it's one of the most frequent uses: vertical content, reels and pieces designed specifically for social media consumption.
Do you also handle the editing?
Yes, editing, color, sound and motion graphics are included — we don't deliver unfinished raw footage.
Can we hire production only?
Yes, if you already have an in-house editing team we can take care of just the filming and creative direction.
Do you run advertising campaigns?
Yes, we produce pieces designed for paid campaigns, coordinated with the Growth & Marketing team when the project requires it.
Can you create different formats from one production?
Yes, from a single session we usually adapt multiple pieces: reel, story, horizontal and vertical cuts, to make better use of each production.
Do you work with AI-generated content?
We evaluate it case by case. We prioritize real production, but there are specific processes (such as some adaptations or proofs of concept) where AI can add value without replacing the original production.

What do you want to build?

Tell us what you want to achieve, what you want to improve or what's blocking your business's growth. We'll analyze the project and propose the next step.
