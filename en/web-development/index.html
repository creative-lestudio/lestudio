02 · Business

Business

Automated processes.

For businesses that want to automate inquiries, orders, bookings or requests without processing payments.

Includes

  • Everything essential from the Starter plan
  • Advanced forms with order or booking logic
  • Automatic orders via WhatsApp or email
  • Calculation of products, extras or quantities when applicable
  • Confirmations and basic automations

03 · E-commerce

E-commerce

Orders + payments.

For businesses that process orders and payments directly from their website through a secure payment gateway.

Includes

  • Catalog, cart and checkout
  • Payment gateway
  • Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay when the platform allows it
  • Automatic order and payment confirmations
  • Backend, webhooks and security measures when needed
  • Basic order management

04 · Commerce Integration

Commerce Integration

Connected ecosystem. Quoted per project.

For businesses that need the website to be part of their sales and operations ecosystem. Quoted per project.

Includes

  • Orders and payments connected to internal systems
  • POS integration
  • APIs, webhooks and advanced automations
  • Multi-location flows when needed
  • Synchronization with inventory, CRM, ERP or other tools
  • Technical architecture adapted to the business

How we choose the plan

The plan doesn't depend on how many pages the website has, but on what it needs to do: showcase, capture, automate, charge or integrate with the business's systems.
