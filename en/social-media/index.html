From trying the service to delegating a large part of your digital ecosystem. We choose the level based on what your business needs to achieve on social media.

01 · Starter

Starter

Entry plan to try the service.

Designed as an entry plan to try the service.

Includes

  • Initial profile audit
  • Basic optimization
  • Monthly planning
  • Content calendar
  • Basic visual direction
  • Basic scripts/copy
  • Design and adaptation of pieces
  • 6–8 content pieces
  • 2 simple reels
  • Stories
  • Publishing/scheduling
  • Basic photo retouching
  • End-of-month report

Does not include: ongoing community management, ads, influencers or advanced strategy. Available only once per client.

Basic

03 · Growth

Growth

Social media as a growth and acquisition tool.

For businesses that want to use social media as a growth and acquisition tool.

Frequently asked questions

Which social networks do you manage?
Mainly Instagram and TikTok, although we adapt to the platform where your audience is. We define it together based on your brand and your goal; we don't apply the same formula to every client.
Do you also produce the content?
Yes. Strategy and production go hand in hand within this service, and if you need more elaborate pieces, it connects directly with Content Production with no friction between teams.
Can we hire strategy only?
Yes, it's possible. Many clients already have an in-house community management team and only need the strategic definition and the calendar. We'll talk it through based on your case.
Do you manage comments and messages?
Yes, community management includes replying to comments and direct messages within the hours and tone we define together for your brand.
How much content is published per month?
It depends on the plan you choose. The three levels — basic, intermediate and advanced — include different numbers of posts, reels and stories, detailed in the plans section.
How do you measure results?
With monthly reports showing reach, engagement and real growth, explained in plain language — not just a screenshot of metrics with no context.
Do you work with brands that are just starting out?
Yes. In fact that's where having a clear strategy from day one adds the most value, instead of posting without a defined direction.

What do you want to build?

Tell us what you want to achieve, what you want to improve or what's blocking your business's growth. We'll analyze the project and propose the next step.
