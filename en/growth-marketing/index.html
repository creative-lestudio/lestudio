Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between SEO and GEO?
SEO works on your ranking in traditional search engines like Google. GEO aims for your brand to be well represented inside the answers of generative AI systems. They're complementary, not mutually exclusive.
Do you work with Google Ads?
Yes, we design, launch and optimize search and shopping campaigns according to each business's goal.
Do you also manage Meta Ads?
Yes, Instagram and Facebook campaigns, coordinated with the rest of the content and brand strategy.
Can you combine SEO and advertising?
Yes, in fact we recommend it: organic builds a sustainable base while paid delivers more immediate results.
What does appearing in AI search engines mean?
It means that when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini or a similar system something related to your industry, your brand is more likely to be mentioned or considered in that answer. It can't be guaranteed at any given moment, but it can be worked on consistently.
How do you measure results?
With periodic analytics and reporting on traffic, rankings, conversion and the performance of each campaign.
Do you work with companies that already have a marketing team?
Yes, on several projects we work as a specialist team that complements the in-house team, not as a replacement.
