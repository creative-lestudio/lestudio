Privacy policy

Here we explain, in plain words, what data we collect when you contact us and what we do with it. Last updated: November 2026.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is LE STUDIO. You can write to us at creative.lestudio@gmail.com or call us on +34 662 490 547 for any question about your data.

2. What data we collect

  • Contact form and audit booking: name, email, phone (optional), company (optional), service of interest, message and, if you tell us, the time slot that suits you for the call.
  • Reviews: name, company, role, rating and review text.
  • Visit source: the page you landed on, the site you came from and the campaign parameters (UTM) if any. We keep them only during your session and attach them to the form to know which channel works.
  • WhatsApp and calls: if you message us on WhatsApp or call us, we process the data you give us in that conversation.

3. What we use it for

  • To reply to your request and send you a proposal.
  • To schedule and run the free 30-minute audit.
  • To moderate and, with your permission, publish your review.
  • To learn which channel contacts come from so we can improve our communication.

4. Legal basis

We process your data with your consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR), which you give by ticking the form checkbox. You can withdraw it at any time by writing to us; it won't affect what has already been done.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. To receive your forms we use a form-handling provider (Formspree) and, if you message us on WhatsApp, that communication goes through WhatsApp (Meta). These providers act on our behalf and may be outside the European Union.

6. How long we keep it

While we have a relationship with you or until you ask us to delete it. Afterwards we block it for as long as the law requires and then delete it.

7. Your rights

You can ask us for access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability by writing to our email. If you think we haven't handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

8. Cookies and browser storage

This site stores in your browser only what is needed to remember your language, the light or dark theme and the origin of your visit during the session. If we enable analytics tools, they will only load if you accept them in the cookie notice.
